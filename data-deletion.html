User Data Deletion

Last updated: January 2026

How to request deletion: If you want us to delete your personal data, follow the steps below.
Step 1 — Send a request
  • Email: info@thecre8hub.com
  • Subject: Data Deletion Request
  • Include: phone number/email used on the service, and any relevant identifiers.
Step 2 — Verification

To protect users, we may ask for additional information to confirm ownership of the account before deletion.

Step 3 — Completion
  • We delete or anonymize personal data where feasible.
  • We may retain limited records where required by law (e.g., payment references for auditing).
  • We confirm completion by email.
For more detail, please see our Privacy Policy.
