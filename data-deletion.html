User Data Deletion

Last updated: January 2026

If you used BMS Creative Concept services and would like us to delete your personal data, you can request deletion using the steps below.

How to request deletion

  • Email us at: info@thecre8hub.com
  • Subject: Data Deletion Request
  • Include: your email/phone used on our service and any relevant account identifiers

What happens next

  • We confirm ownership of the request.
  • We delete or anonymize personal data unless retention is required by law (e.g., payment records).
  • We respond to confirm completion.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
