About the service

Naija Hustle Tax Guide is a subscription-based digital service built by BMS Creative Concept. The service provides clear educational guidance on Nigerian taxes, record keeping, and compliance basics for individuals, creators, freelancers and small businesses.

Our mission
  • Make tax knowledge simple and practical.
  • Help small businesses and hustlers stay compliant.
  • Reduce confusion with step-by-step guidance.
What we are (and are not)
  • We provide educational guidance and structured explanations.
  • We do not claim to be a government agency.
  • We do not replace licensed tax/legal professionals.
Service delivery
  • WhatsApp: users interact with the assistant and receive guidance messages.
  • Web: users can access policies, pricing, and support pages, plus helpful resources.
  • Support: email support for subscription and account issues.
Compliance & transparency
  • We maintain clear Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
  • Refund rules are stated in our Refund Policy.
  • We provide a User Data Deletion page for user requests.
