How it works

This page explains exactly how customers use our services, how payments are processed, and how access is delivered after payment. This clarity is important for payment and compliance reviews.

1

Choose a plan

Customers review our service plans and select the option that matches their needs.

2

Pay securely via Paystack

Payments are processed securely by Paystack. We do not collect or store card details.

3

Access is granted

After successful payment, customers receive confirmation and access to the subscribed digital tools.

4

Service delivery

Depending on the plan, delivery may include web access and/or messaging-based assistance workflows.

5

Support & billing help

Customers can contact support for access issues, billing questions, or service clarification.

6

Cancellation & refunds

Rules for cancellations and refunds are defined clearly in the Refund Policy and Terms.

Important notes

BMS Creative Concept is a digital service provider. Payments are for online services and subscription access. Please review our legal pages for full details.
