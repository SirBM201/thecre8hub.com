Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Summary: Subscriptions provide instant access to a digital service. Refunds may be granted only in specific cases described below. This policy is designed to be clear and fair.
Eligible refund cases
  • Duplicate charge: If you were charged twice for the same subscription, we will refund the duplicate amount.
  • Service access failure: If you paid but could not access the service and we cannot resolve it within a reasonable time.
  • Incorrect billing: If you were billed the wrong amount due to a technical error.
Non-refundable cases
  • Change of mind after using the service.
  • Refund requests after substantial service use within an active plan.
  • Refund requests based on user misunderstanding of educational guidance.
How to request a refund
  • Email: info@thecre8hub.com
  • Subject: Refund Request
  • Include: payer name, payment date, amount, and payment reference/receipt.
Processing timeline
  • Review: within 3–7 business days.
  • Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method where possible.
  • Bank processing times may vary depending on the payment provider.
Need help? Contact support via Contact before purchasing if you have questions about the service.
