Refund Policy

Last updated: January 5, 2026

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which BMS Creative Concept may provide refunds for digital services, subscriptions, and related payments. Our services are primarily delivered digitally (including subscription-based access), and refunds are therefore limited to specific cases described below.

1. General Rule: Digital Services

Because our services are delivered digitally and may be accessed immediately after purchase, refunds are generally not provided once access has been granted and the service has been used. This approach helps protect the platform against abuse while maintaining fair access for all users.

2. Refunds We May Approve

We may approve a refund only in the following situations:

  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same subscription or order.
  • Payment error: your payment was successful but your subscription was not activated due to a technical issue on our side.
  • Service failure: a significant technical problem prevented you from accessing the purchased service for reasons attributable to us.
Refund window: Any eligible refund request must be submitted within 24 hours of the payment date. Requests submitted after this window may be declined.

3. When Refunds Are Not Provided

Refunds will not be provided in these common scenarios:

  • You changed your mind after purchase.
  • You did not use the service during the subscription period.
  • Your device, internet connection, WhatsApp availability, or third-party platform restrictions prevent you from using the service.
  • You violated our Terms of Use, including misuse, abuse, or prohibited activities.
  • Your account was limited or suspended due to policy violations or security reasons.

4. No Pro-Rated or Partial Refunds

For subscription plans (monthly, quarterly, yearly), we do not provide pro-rated or partial refunds for unused time once the subscription has started, except where required by applicable law.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund (if eligible), you must provide:

  • Your payment reference / transaction reference
  • The phone number associated with the account
  • A clear explanation of the issue
  • Any screenshots or supporting evidence (if available)

6. Refund Processing Time

If approved, refunds are processed back to the original payment method where possible. Processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

7. Policy Changes

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
