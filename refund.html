Refund Policy

Last updated: January 5, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how BMS Creative Concept handles refunds for digital services, subscriptions, and payments made through our platforms.

1. Digital Services

Our services are delivered digitally, including subscription-based access. Once access is granted and the service is used, refunds are generally not provided.

2. When a Refund May Be Approved

Refunds may be approved only in limited situations such as:

  • Duplicate payment for the same service or subscription
  • Payment successful but subscription not activated due to our system error
  • Major technical failure that prevented service access and is attributable to us

Refund requests must be submitted within 24 hours of payment. Requests outside this window may be declined.

3. When Refunds Are Not Granted

  • Change of mind after purchase
  • Failure to use the service during the subscription period
  • Issues caused by internet connection, devices, WhatsApp availability, or third-party restrictions
  • Account suspension due to violation of our Terms of Use

4. No Partial or Pro-Rated Refunds

Subscription plans (monthly, quarterly, yearly) are not eligible for partial or pro-rated refunds once the subscription period has started, except where required by law.

5. How to Request a Refund

If eligible, you must provide:

  • Payment or transaction reference
  • The phone number linked to your account
  • A clear explanation of the issue

6. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method where possible. Processing time depends on your bank or payment provider.
