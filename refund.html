Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to payments made for BMS Creative Concept digital services and subscription-based access plans.

2. When refunds may be granted

  • Duplicate payment for the same plan within a short timeframe.
  • Payment completed but service access was not provided due to a verified technical error.
  • Other exceptional cases assessed by support.

3. When refunds are not granted

  • Change of mind after service access has already been provided.
  • Misuse of service or violation of Terms.
  • Failure to use the service during an active subscription period.

4. How to request a refund

  • Email: info@thecre8hub.com
  • Include your payment reference, email/phone used, and a short description of the issue.
Processing: If approved, refunds are processed via the original payment channel where possible. Timelines depend on payment method and provider processing times.

5. Contact

If you have questions about refunds, please contact support via the Contact page.
