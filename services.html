Services

BMS Creative Concept provides digital services and subscription-based support tools. Below is a clear breakdown of what we offer, how customers receive value, and the type of payments accepted.

1) Subscription-based digital tools

Access structured tools and resources designed to support users with guided information and step-by-step assistance.

  • Premium access to structured guidance content and tool features
  • Plan-based access limits (monthly / quarterly / yearly)
  • Service updates and improvements during subscription period

2) Messaging-based assistance (WhatsApp workflow)

Where applicable, customers can interact through WhatsApp-based workflows to receive service outputs.

  • User-initiated requests only (no unsolicited messaging)
  • Automated responses and guided prompts
  • Support escalations via email when needed

3) Advisory & informational digital products

We provide structured informational digital deliverables aligned with the purchased plan and scope.

  • Digital guidance content and checklists
  • Structured templates and reference materials
  • Plan-based customer support

4) Customer support & billing help

Customers can reach us for billing, access issues, and service questions.

  • Email support during business hours
  • Clear cancellation guidance
  • Refund handling as defined in the Refund Policy

Payments & delivery

  • Payment: Processed securely via Paystack. We do not store customer card details.
  • Delivery: Access to digital services is provided after successful payment and confirmation.
  • Documentation: See Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Refund Policy.
